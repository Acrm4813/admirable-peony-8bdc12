About Ledger

Ledger keeps your money in one place: what comes in, what goes out, and what you have invested. Your net worth is your cash plus the market value of your holdings.

Accounts. Anyone can create an account with an email and password. Each account’s data is stored privately and can only be read by its owner.

Live information. Stocks and ETFs come from Finnhub, crypto from CoinGecko and exchange rates from Frankfurter. The Markets tab refreshes every minute while the app is open. Free data feeds can be delayed, so check your broker before you trade.

Investing. Ledger records trades you enter. It can’t place real orders. Trade with your broker, then log it in Invest so your totals stay accurate.

Ledger is a tracking tool, not financial advice.
